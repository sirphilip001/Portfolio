About me

Hi, I'm Philip

PK

I'm a data analyst based in Nairobi, working at Data Science East Africa since June 2025. I hold a Data Science and Analytics certification from LuxDevHQ, where I'm also deepening my skills in data science and AI.

My work starts with SQL: pulling, joining and cleaning data so it can be trusted. From there I use Power BI and Python to turn it into dashboards and insights people can actually use.

I'm ambitious and practical. I like learning by building real projects, and I'm growing toward AI and machine learning.
